This is a morning's bank file. Sixty receipts. Dana's job is to decide which supplier invoice each one settles. [[slnc 380]] Most of it is boring. The problem is the tail — a receipt short by a bank fee, one wire covering four invoices, a supplier name the bank truncated so it matches two vendors. [[slnc 380]] Clearing an easy receipt saves a minute. Posting a hard one against the wrong invoice means two balances are now wrong. So Dana goes slowly on everything, because the expensive mistakes look exactly like the cheap ones until after you make them.
